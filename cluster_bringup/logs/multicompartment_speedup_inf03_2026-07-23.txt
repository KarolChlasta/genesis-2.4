Workload: N=200 neurons, NCOMP=16 (3200 compartments), single hsolve, Na+K per compartment.
Method: two-step-count subtraction (S1=5000, S2=25000; construction cancels).

CPU (chanmode 1, fp64, nxgenesis_nocl): dwall=5.947 s / 20000 steps = 297.4 us/step
CUDA (chanmode 4, A100, nxgenesis):     dwall=0.062 s / 20000 steps = 3.1 us/step
CUDA kernel self-report (S2):           25010 steps, kernel 59.5 ms = 2.378 us/step

COMPUTE SPEEDUP (CPU/CUDA) = ~96x   (consistent with Arbor ~200x/GPU literature)

Caveats: single measurement, one size, A100 only. Full campaign (reps+CI,
NCOMP/N sweep, A40 vs A100) pending. Earlier single-compartment "no speedup"
was confounded by an argv bug (N_STEPS not read) + a non-compute-bound workload.
